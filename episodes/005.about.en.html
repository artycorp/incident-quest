What is Transloadit

Transloadit is a media processing platform for developers (MPaaS, Media Processing as a Service): it encodes video, resizes images, and handles file imports and exports. Three engineers founded the company in 2009: Kevin van Zonneveld, Tim Koschützki and Felix Geisendörfer. The team is small and spread across several countries.

Three things matter for this episode:

  • Assemblies. A processing job is called an Assembly: a customer starts it through the API, and workers pick it up from a queue and run it.
  • Shared Redis. A region’s queues, locks and metadata live on shared Redis servers. That is convenient, but when Redis is unavailable every role suffers at once.
  • Regions. The service runs in several AWS regions; the incident hit us-east-1.
