# LinkedIn (EN)

One hundred phones for 1 zloty. Twenty minutes of Allegro being down.

On July 18, 2018, Poland’s Allegro ran a noon sale: Honor 7C phones that normally cost about 850 zloty, for 1 zloty. Traffic hit 200–300% of the day before and the site went down, while the data centers had plenty of idle CPU and RAM.

It’s 11:57, response times are climbing, and you are on call. What do you do?

I turned Allegro’s public postmortem into an interactive investigation: pick your next move at every step, read the dashboards, and see where each wrong turn leads. Watch for the moment latency drops in the middle of the outage.
