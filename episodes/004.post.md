# LinkedIn (EN)

A cheaper database on Saturday. Six hours of downtime on Monday.

In August 2016 Buildkite was running out of AWS credits, so over a weekend the team moved its main PostgreSQL to a smaller instance and put one shared PgBouncer in front of it. Nobody load tested it. On Monday, when US customers woke up, buildkite.com went down, while builds kept running and fresh servers kept booting and dying.

It's 21:00 UTC, the site has been down since 17:21, and you are on call. Where do you start?

I turned Buildkite's public postmortem into an interactive investigation: pick your next move at every step, read the dashboards, and see where each wrong turn leads. Watch what the health check does once the database slows down.

If you played the earlier episodes, look closely: there is something new in the steps.
