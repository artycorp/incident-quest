What is Allegro

Allegro is Poland’s largest online marketplace, roughly a Polish eBay: individual sellers and shops list offers, buyers search for them and pay on the site. The company was founded in 1999 in Poznań.

Three things matter for this episode:

  • Scale. Allegro is one of the most visited sites in the country, so a 1-zloty sale pulls traffic from all over Poland within minutes.
  • The zloty is Poland’s currency. In 2018 one zloty was worth about €0.23; the sale phone normally cost about 850 zloty.
  • Its own infrastructure. In 2018 the site ran as a set of microservices in the company’s own private cloud, not a public cloud. Allegro engineers write about it on the Allegro Tech Blog, where the postmortem of this outage was published.
